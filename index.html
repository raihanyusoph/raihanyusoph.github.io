Research focus

Peace is experienced locally.

My work connects institutional agreements with what happens in communities: who benefits, who remains insecure, and how people remember and navigate conflict.

01

Bangsamoro history

Political and socio-cultural histories of Mindanao, with particular attention to Mranaw communities, historical memory, and the institutions that shape belonging.

02

Peace, conflict & security

Research on peace agreements, clan conflict, firearms, local justice, and the everyday conditions that determine whether peace becomes tangible.

03

Development after agreement

Field-based analysis of assistance, governance, and the persistent gap between formal peace settlements and community-level peace dividends.

Guiding inquiry

What happens when peace agreements change institutions, but insecurity continues to shape everyday life?

This question runs through my research on development programs, rido, firearms, historical injustice, and community memory across the Bangsamoro.
