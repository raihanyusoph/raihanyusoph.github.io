Teaching & engagement

Teach history. Examine power. Practice peace.

My teaching helps students connect historical evidence and conceptual questions with the lived realities of Mindanao and the Bangsamoro.

Teaching philosophy

Learning through evidence, context, and dialogue

Historical understanding becomes most useful when students can examine evidence critically, locate events in context, and relate competing interpretations to present public questions.

In the classroom and in practitioner training, I emphasize accessible explanation without sacrificing analytical depth. Students are invited to ask who produced a narrative, what evidence sustains it, whose experiences remain marginal, and how knowledge can contribute to more responsible public action.

Public engagement

From the university to peacebuilding practice

I am available for academic lectures, research seminars, conference panels, and carefully aligned educational and peacebuilding engagements.
